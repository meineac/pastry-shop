Privacy Policy

Effective date: 01/01/2025

This Privacy Policy explains how Pastry Shop (a.k.a. “we”, “our”, “us”) collects, uses, and protects the personal data of customers who use our website and services. We are committed to protecting your privacy in accordance with the GDPR and applicable UK data protection law.

  • Data We Collect
  • How We Use Your Data
  • Data Sharing
  • Data Retention
  • Your Rights
  • Cookies
  • Contact Us

1. Data We Collect

When you use our website and services, we may collect the following categories of personal data:

Account Information
Your username, first name, last name, email address, date of birth, and phone number when you register.
Order Data
Details of products ordered, delivery preferences, payment method, and order history.
Usage Data
Your IP address, browser type, pages visited, and time spent on the site.
Review Data
Text reviews and ratings you submit for products or the store.
Communication Data
Any messages you send us via the contact form or email.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your personal data for the following purposes:

  • To process and fulfil your orders for pastries and confectionery.
  • To manage your customer account and order history.
  • To send order confirmation and delivery notifications.
  • To respond to your enquiries and support requests.
  • To improve our website, products, and services based on usage patterns.
  • To send promotional emails about seasonal specials, new products, and loyalty rewards — only if you have opted in.
  • To comply with legal obligations, including food safety and consumer protection law.

Legal basis: Processing is necessary for the performance of a contract (order fulfilment), our legitimate interests (service improvement), and your consent (marketing communications).

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We may share your data with:

  • Delivery partners — your name and delivery address to fulfil your order.
  • Payment processors — for secure handling of card payments (PCI-DSS compliant).
  • Cloud hosting providers — for secure data storage (servers located in the EEA).
  • Law enforcement — only where required by law.

4. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. Order data is retained for 7 years to comply with tax and financial reporting obligations. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time (see Your Rights below).

5. Your Rights

Under the GDPR, you have the following rights:

  • Right of access — request a copy of your personal data.
  • Right to rectification — request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Right to erasure — request deletion of your personal data (“right to be forgotten”).
  • Right to restrict processing — request that we limit how we use your data.
  • Right to data portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to object — object to processing based on legitimate interests or for direct marketing.
  • Right to withdraw consent — unsubscribe from marketing at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact our Data Protection Officer (see Contact section).

6. Cookies

We use only strictly necessary cookies to maintain your session (login state and shopping cart). We do not use tracking, advertising, or third-party analytics cookies. You can control cookies in your browser settings; disabling session cookies may prevent login and cart functionality.
