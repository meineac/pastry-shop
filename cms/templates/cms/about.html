Company Details

{{ company_info.company_details }}

We are proud to hold the HACCP certification, ensuring the highest food safety standards in all our production processes.

Our range includes viennoiserie — yeast-leavened, laminated doughs such as croissants and danishes — and entremets — multi-layered mousse cakes assembled with precision and mirror glazes.

All products comply with EU food labelling regulations and are produced under GMP guidelines.
